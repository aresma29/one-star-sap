A thirsty houseplant leaves a one-star review for its forgetful human, who saves all his apologies for the plastic plant instead. The joke lands in the contrast between the living plant still waiting and the fake one getting all the attention. It is short, dry, and stubbornly hopeful in the worst way — a complaint that refuses to wilt.

> One star. My human waters me when he remembers, which is never, then apologizes to the plastic plant. I am still here. I am still thirsty.

Run `main.py` to print the entry line from `entry.txt`.
